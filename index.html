About Lunar Codex

Twelve living lunar reflections

Each Moon Clock is a complete, self-contained interactive experience designed around one zodiac sign’s unique relationship to the lunar cycle.

They share the same underlying engine (live lunar phase calculation, 8-phase wheel, birth phase lookup) but are deliberately reskinned with distinct visual languages, ambient motion, color palettes, and poetic guidance tailored to the sign.

How to use:
• Open any clock in a new tab
• Tap phases on the wheel or in the grid to explore
• Use the “My Birth Phase” button for personal insight
• The lunar hand and center visual update in real time
